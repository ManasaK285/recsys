# academic_integrity
Starter note - verify and replace with vetted sources before relying on it.

Automated AI-text detectors are considered unreliable for high-stakes decisions. Liang et al. (2023, Patterns) reported that several detectors misclassified writing by non-native English speakers as AI-generated, so detection-based enforcement can create unfair outcomes for multilingual students.

Many institutions instead redesign assessments, require disclosure of AI use, and use process evidence such as drafts and oral follow-ups to protect academic integrity.
