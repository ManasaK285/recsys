FERPA protects the privacy of student education records in the US, and COPPA regulates online services that collect personal information from children under 13. Sending student writing or identifiers to third-party AI services can raise student data privacy questions that a policy should address, such as vendor contracts and data retention.

Common safeguards include data-processing agreements, disabling training on student data, and minimizing personal information in prompts.
