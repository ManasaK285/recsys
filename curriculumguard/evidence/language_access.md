# language_access
Starter note - verify and replace with vetted sources before relying on it.

US civil-rights law and precedent (for example Lau v. Nichols, 1974, and the Equal Educational Opportunities Act) require schools to take steps so English learners can participate meaningfully. Translation and language-support tools can serve that goal, while restricting them may reduce access for multilingual students and families.

Machine translation errors are a known risk, so human review matters for important communications.
